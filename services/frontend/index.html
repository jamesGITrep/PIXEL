PixelMosaic: Turn Thousands of Photos Into One Giant Photo Mosaic

PixelMosaic builds high-resolution photo mosaics from your own pictures and crowd-sourced photos, and powers live event walls where every guest's photo becomes a tile in a shared artwork, in real time.

Live Event Arena

A live photo mosaic wall for festivals, weddings, conferences and stages. Guests scan a QR code and upload a photo from their phone; it appears on the big screen as part of the mosaic within seconds, and "Find My Photo" shows each guest where their picture landed.

Photo Mosaic Studio

Upload a picture and a folder of photos, and the studio rebuilds it as a mosaic using perceptual colour matching, with no repeated photos side by side. Mix your own photos with the community library, filter by theme, and export a print-ready image up to 12,000 pixels.
